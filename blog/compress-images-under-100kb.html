How to Compress Images to Under 100KB (Free Online Tool)

Published: [add date] · 5 min read

Large image files slow down your website, hurt your SEO rankings, and frustrate visitors. In this guide, I'll show you exactly how to compress any image to under 100KB using a free online tool — no software installation required.

Why 100KB Matters for Your Website

Google uses page speed as a ranking factor. Studies show that 53% of mobile users abandon a site that takes longer than 3 seconds to load. Images are usually the biggest culprit...

Step-by-Step: Compress to 100KB

  1. Open ImageResizer Pro.
  2. Upload your image or capture one with your camera.
  3. Set the quality slider to around 60-70%.
  4. Set Max Size to 100 KB.
  5. Adjust dimensions if needed (often 1200px wide is enough).
  6. Choose JPEG or WebP for best compression.
  7. Click "Compress Image" and download the result.

Frequently Asked Questions

Will compression reduce image quality? Slightly, but at 60-70% quality most photos look identical to the human eye.

Which format compresses best? WebP compresses best, followed by JPEG. PNG is best only for graphics with transparency.

About ImageToolkit

ImageToolkit was built to give everyone a fast, private, and free way to process images directly in the browser. No uploads to a server, no accounts, and no hidden fees—just powerful tools that run on your device using modern web APIs.

What You Can Do

  • 🔍 Extract text from images with OCR (Tesseract.js)
  • ✂️ Crop images precisely with Cropper.js
  • ✨ Adjust brightness, contrast, and saturation
  • 📦 Compress images before sharing
  • ✏️ Add styled text overlays with Fabric.js
  • 📷 Capture photos directly from your camera
  • 🔎 Hand off images to Google Lens for visual search

Why It’s Different

Every operation happens locally in your browser. Your images never leave your device, which makes ImageToolkit ideal for sensitive documents, personal photos, and quick edits on the go. The site is fully responsive and works on desktop, tablet, and mobile.

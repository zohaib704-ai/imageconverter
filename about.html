About ImageResizer Pro

ImageResizer Pro is a collection of free, browser-based image tools built for anyone who needs to compress, edit, enhance, or read text from photos — without signups, uploads, or limits.

Why We Built This

Most online image tools make you wait for uploads, force you to create an account, or watermark your output. We wanted something different: a set of professional-grade tools that run entirely in your browser, respect your privacy, and never cost a cent.

What You Can Do Here

  • Compress images to a target file size (MB to KB) for faster websites and smaller email attachments.
  • Enhance & Clear dull or blurry photos with auto-levels, real sharpening, and denoising.
  • Crop and resize with draggable corners — exactly like your phone's gallery editor.
  • Apply filters, rotate, flip, and upscale.
  • Add text, captions, and watermarks.
  • Read text from images (OCR) in 8 languages — and translate it instantly.
  • Take photos with a 1×–10× zoom camera, right in the browser.

Our Privacy Promise

Every tool on this site runs 100% on your device using JavaScript and WebAssembly. Your photos are never uploaded to any server, never stored, and never seen by us. Close the tab and everything is gone.

Free, Forever

There are no accounts, no paywalls, and no hidden fees. The site is supported by non-intrusive advertising. If you find these tools useful, sharing them with a friend is the best way to say thanks.

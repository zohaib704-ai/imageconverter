Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2026

1. Images You Process

All image processing on ImageResizer Pro — compression, editing, enhancement, cropping, filters, text overlay, upscaling, and OCR — happens entirely in your browser. Your images are never uploaded to any server, never stored, and never viewed by us. When you close the tab, everything is gone.

2. Cookies and Advertising

This site may display advertising to keep the tools free. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.

You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings, or opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting aboutads.info.

3. Contact Form

If you submit the contact form, your name, email, subject, and message are sent to us via Web3Forms (a third-party email service). This information is used only to reply to your message. It is never sold, shared, or used for marketing.

4. Analytics

We may use privacy-friendly analytics to understand aggregate visitor numbers (like page views). This does not identify individual users.

5. Third-Party Libraries

The site loads open-source libraries (Tesseract.js for OCR, Cropper.js for cropping) from public CDNs. These CDNs may log your IP address as part of normal web traffic, in accordance with their own privacy policies.

6. Children's Privacy

This site is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.

8. Contact

Questions about this policy? Send us a message.

These networks may use cookies (including the DoubleClick DART cookie) to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.

Our contact form is powered by Formspree. When you submit a message, your name, email, and message are transmitted to Formspree and forwarded to us. We use this information solely to respond to your inquiry.

4. Analytics

We may use privacy‑respecting analytics to understand aggregate traffic patterns. No personally identifiable information is collected.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to request deletion of any personal data you have submitted. Contact us via the contact page to exercise this right.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
